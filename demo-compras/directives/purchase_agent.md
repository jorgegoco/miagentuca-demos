# Purchase Agent Directive

## Purpose
Assist any business with procurement by searching suppliers, comparing prices, and generating purchase recommendations. The agent auto-detects the product category and selects appropriate Spanish suppliers.

## Input
- **Product description**: What the user wants to buy (e.g., "1 paquete de folios A4", "100 tornillos de 6mm acero inoxidable", "caja de guantes de nitrilo")
- **Quantity**: How many units needed
- **Urgency**: normal, urgent, very_urgent

## Process

### Step 1: Parse Request
Extract from user input:
- Product type and specifications
- Auto-detect product category
- Quantity needed
- Delivery urgency
- Any brand preferences

### Step 2: Search Suppliers
Use AI to simulate supplier search. Suppliers must be:
- Real Spanish companies appropriate for the product category
- Category-specific (auto-detected from the product description)

Supplier pools by category:
- Material de oficina: Lyreco, Staples, RAJA España, Office Depot, Amazon Business
- Informática/tecnología: PcComponentes, Amazon Business, Esprinet, LDLC, Coolmod
- Hostelería/alimentación: Makro, GM Food, Miró, Distriplus, Coviran
- Limpieza/higiene: Papelmatic, Proquimia, Distriplus, Amazon Business, Leroy Merlin Pro
- Tornillería/fijaciones: Würth España, Bricomart, Leroy Merlin Pro, Saltoki, Coferdroza
- Herramientas eléctricas: Würth España, Leroy Merlin Pro, Bricomart, Saltoki, Makita España
- Material eléctrico: Rexel, Saltoki, Grupo Electro Stocks, Sonepar, Leroy Merlin Pro
- Construcción/albañilería: Bricomart, BigMat, Leroy Merlin Pro, Punto de la Construcción, Cemex
- Fontanería: Saltoki, Salvador Escoda, Leroy Merlin Pro, Bricomart, Roca
- Pintura: Bricomart, Leroy Merlin Pro, AkzoNobel, Pinturas Isaval, Jotun
- Otro (productos generales): Amazon Business, ManoMano, Leroy Merlin Pro, Alibaba España, RAJA España

### Step 3: Compare Options
For each supplier, evaluate:
- Unit price
- Bulk discounts
- Delivery time
- Minimum order quantity
- Shipping cost

### Step 4: Generate Recommendation
Provide:
- Best price option
- Best delivery time option
- Best overall value (balanced, urgency-aware scoring)
- Context-aware reasoning that includes:
  - Urgency context when applicable ("Dado la urgencia del pedido...")
  - Explicit note when best_price and fastest_delivery are the same supplier
  - Total savings comparison for large quantity orders (>50 units)
- 2-3 procurement tips specific to the product category

## Urgency-Aware Behavior
- **normal**: Standard scoring — price weighted equally with delivery time.
- **urgent / very_urgent**: The prompt explicitly instructs Claude to prioritize in-stock suppliers and fast delivery. The value score penalizes suppliers with delivery > 2 days more heavily. Reasoning is prefixed with urgency context.

## Bulk Order Behavior
- For quantities > 50, the prompt instructs Claude to highlight bulk discount availability and minimum order requirements.
- The reasoning includes total savings between cheapest and most expensive supplier.

## Procurement Tips
- Generated by Claude as part of the supplier search response.
- 2-3 short, practical, category-specific tips (e.g., "Los folios A4 de 80g son suficientes para impresoras láser estándar" or "Compre guantes de nitrilo en cajas de 1000 uds para ahorros del 15-20%").
- Optional field with empty list default for backward compatibility.

## Price Ranges
- Each supplier includes a `price_range` with `low` and `high` values reflecting real market variability.
- Stable products (paper, bleach, standard screws) have narrow ranges (10-15% spread).
- Volatile products (olive oil, tech) have wider ranges (15-25% spread).
- The `unit_price` must always fall within the range (ideally near the midpoint).
- Optional field — backward compatible if missing.

## Price Confidence
- `price_confidence` indicates the reliability of the price estimate:
  - `"estimated"` — based on general market knowledge (default)
  - `"reference"` — based on known catalog or published data
- Optional field, defaults to `"estimated"`.

## Procurement Strategy
- Mandatory 3-5 sentence paragraph with strategic purchasing advice specific to the product and quantity.
- Must include at least 2 of: consumption patterns, volume discount opportunities, quality/alternative considerations, timing recommendations, alternative product suggestions.
- Professional consultant tone — demonstrates business intelligence beyond simple price comparison.
- Optional field in the response model (defaults to `None`) for backward compatibility.

## Estimated Date
- `estimated_as_of` contains the month and year (in Spanish) when the price estimates were generated.
- Example: "Febrero 2025", "Marzo 2025".
- Optional field, defaults to `None`.

## Edge Cases

### No suppliers found
Return empty suppliers array with helpful message.

### Product unclear
Ask for clarification or make reasonable assumptions, noting them in response.

### Very large quantities
Flag that bulk/wholesale pricing may apply and recommend direct contact.

## Price Accuracy
The system prompt includes reference price ranges for many product categories (office supplies, tech, hospitality, cleaning, screws, power tools, cables, construction, plumbing, paint). Claude must generate prices WITHIN these ranges. If a product doesn't appear in the references, Claude extrapolates from similar products. This was added to fix unrealistic pricing issues discovered during testing.

**Price spread**: Prices must NOT be clustered within 10% of each other. Generalist suppliers (Amazon Business, Leroy Merlin) are typically 15-30% more expensive than specialists. The most expensive supplier should be at least 25-40% more than the cheapest.

**Stock behavior**: Stock availability varies naturally by product type. High-rotation products (paper, standard screws, bleach) are almost always in stock. Specialized or branded products have a real chance of being out of stock. There is no forced "at least one out of stock" rule.

## Spanish Context
- All prices in EUR, sin IVA
- Suppliers are real Spanish/European companies matched to product category
- Product names may be in Spanish
- Shipping costs must be coherent with total order weight

## Unit Normalization
Vague inputs are normalized to standard commercial units. Examples:
- "unos guantes" → caja de 100 uds
- "folios" → paquete 500 hojas
- "tornillos M6" → caja de 100 uds
The `unit` field in each supplier response always clarifies what the price refers to.

## Rate Limiting
- 3 requests per minute per IP + 20 requests per day per IP
- Proxy-aware IP detection (X-Forwarded-For / X-Real-IP)
- CORS restricted to miagentuca.es and demo subdomains
- Max query length: 500 characters
- Configurable via `RATE_LIMIT` env var (default: `3/minute;20/day`)
